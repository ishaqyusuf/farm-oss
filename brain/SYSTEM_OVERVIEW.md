# System Overview

## Purpose
This file summarizes what the product is, who it serves, and the initial system boundaries.

## How To Use
- Update when product positioning, user segments, or top-level architecture changes.
- Keep this file short and easy to scan.
- Use it as the first read for new collaborators.

## Product Summary
The product is a farm operations tracker focused on helping farms replace notebook-based record keeping with simple digital workflows.

## Initial Target Market
- Poultry farms as the first and primary MVP audience.
- Small to mid-sized farms in Nigeria and similar markets.
- Farm owners and farm managers who need daily operational visibility.

## Core Jobs To Be Done
- Record daily flock activity quickly.
- Track production, mortality, expenses, and sales.
- Understand farm profitability without manual calculations.
- Surface simple performance trends and alerts.

## MVP Scope
- Flock management
- Daily records
- Expense tracking
- Production tracking
- Sales tracking
- Simple dashboard and alerts

## Non-Goals For MVP
- Full livestock management
- Deep breeding workflows
- Complex IoT integrations
- Heavy ERP-style setup flows

## Platform Assumptions
- SaaS product with multi-tenant support.
- Mobile-first usage with optional web admin.
- Product must remain usable with low technical confidence users.
