# ADR Template

## Purpose
This template is used for Architecture Decision Records in `brain/decisions/`.

## How To Use
- Copy this file into `brain/decisions/` with a numbered filename.
- Use ADRs for decisions that affect long-term architecture or maintenance.
- Keep the record factual and concise.

## Template
# ADR-XXX: Title

## Status
- Proposed

## Context
- What prompted the decision

## Decision
- What was chosen

## Consequences
- Benefits
- Tradeoffs

## Alternatives Considered
- Option A
- Option B
